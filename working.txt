=== TrueEyeball Project Commands ===

To run these commands, you must open your terminal/PowerShell inside your project folder:
cd "c:\Users\tiwar\Desktop\Projects Github\TrueEyeballproject"

--- 1. HOW TO REGISTER A NEW FACE ---
Use this to capture your face and associate it with a name.
Command:
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe register.py

--- 2. HOW TO MANAGE USERS ---
Use this to see who is registered, change names, or delete a profile.

List all users:
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe manage_users.py list

Rename a user:
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe manage_users.py rename "Old_Name" "New_Name"

Delete (revoke) a user:
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe manage_users.py revoke "Name"

--- 3. HOW TO RUN THE APP ---
Use this to actually run the computer vision app. 
(It will only start if at least one person is registered!)
Command:
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py

To close the app safely, click the camera window and press the 'Q' or 'ESC' key!
